    The crew behind it

    The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) at the Institute of Engineering & Management, Kolkata, offers a four-year B.Tech programme focused on machine learning, deep learning, data science and their applications. The department runs dedicated AIML and IoT laboratories and works closely with the IETE Student Forum and the institute's innovation cell.

    Department events are planned by a student committee under faculty guidance. Every event listed on this site is open to students across all branches unless stated otherwise.

    Questions people ask

    Who can take part?

    Any B.Tech student of IEM, from any branch and any year, unless an event says otherwise on its card.

    Is there a fee?

    Most department events are free. Events with a fee show it on the card, and you pay at the registration desk on the day.

    Can I register for more than one event?

    Yes. Submit the form once for each event. You cannot register for the same event twice with the same roll number.

    I cannot make it any more. What do I do?

    Cancel your pass from the list under the registration form, so the seat goes to someone else.

    Do I need to bring anything?

    Your college ID and your pass ID. For hackathons and workshops, bring a laptop and a charger.
